# Prompt Test

Copy one prompt at a time into ErgoLogix to trigger different tools.

## General chat

- I want a quick ergonomic check, but answer normally first and only use a tool if needed.
- What should I do right now to feel better while working?
- Can you help me with my desk setup?
- I need practical advice for working at my computer all day.

## Neck / posture tool

- My neck is stiff after being on my laptop for hours.
- I have cervical pain from looking down at my screen too long.
- My neck and upper back feel tight after a long coding session.
- I have a nape ache from desk work and want to know the risk.

## Wrist tool

- My wrist hurts after typing all day.
- I have ulnar soreness and keyboard strain.
- My carpal area feels irritated from mouse and keyboard use.
- My wrist feels off after a long work session.

## Lower back tool

- My lower back hurts after sitting for 5 hours straight.
- I have lumbar discomfort from working at my desk.
- My back hurts and I want to know the likely ergonomic cause.
- My lower back feels tight after a long coding session.

## Shoulder tool

- My shoulders are sore after sitting at my desk all day.
- I have deltoid tension from bad workstation posture.
- My shoulder feels strained after working on the laptop for hours.
- My shoulder and upper body feel tense from desk work.

## Elbow tool

- My elbow hurts after using the mouse for too long.
- I have epicondylitis-like pain from repeated typing and clicking.
- My elbow feels strained after a long coding session.
- My forearm and elbow are sore from desk work.

## Environmental tool

- It feels hot and humid in here and I am getting sluggish.
- The room is warm, the humidity is high, and I feel fatigued.
- Can weather or heat make me feel worse while I am working?
- It is stuffy and uncomfortable, and I want to know if the environment is affecting me.
- I think the temperature and humidity are making me feel drained.

## Semantic search tool

- What injuries can I get from sitting like this all day?
- What can this posture cause over time?
- Show me the most relevant guide for my back pain.
- Search the guide for the best advice on this discomfort.
- What does this mean for my neck and what should I read next?

## Multi-tool prompts

- I have been sitting for 5 hours, my back hurts, and the room feels hot.
- My neck hurts, my wrist is sore, and I feel drained from the heat.
- I have lower back pain after a long workday and the office feels humid.
- My shoulder and elbow hurt after coding all day; what is going on?

## Strong trigger-style prompts

- I have been sitting for 5 hours straight and my back hurts, what injuries can I get from this?
- I have been typing all day and my wrist and elbow are sore, what does this mean?
- It is hot and humid and I feel tired at my desk, can you analyze that?
- Search for the most relevant ergonomic guidance for my neck pain.

## Short fallback prompts

- Neck pain from desk work.
- Wrist strain after typing.
- Lower back pain from sitting.
- Hot and humid room.
- What injuries can this cause?
