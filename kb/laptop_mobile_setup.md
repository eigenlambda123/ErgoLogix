# Correcting Laptop and Mobile Device Posture

Laptop and phone use often drive a forward head posture, rounded shoulders, and wrist compression. This can lead to neck pain, shoulder tightness, and thumb or hand fatigue by the end of the day.

Raise your laptop with a stand, use an external keyboard and mouse, and hold your phone at eye level when possible. Frequent posture resets prevent cumulative strain from portable device habits.
