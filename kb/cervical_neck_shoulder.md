# Fixing Cervical Neck and Shoulder Tightness

A stiff neck and tight shoulder muscles often result from looking down at a laptop for extended periods. This cervical fatigue quickly leads to a sore neck and general shoulder pain. 

Raise your monitor so the top third of the screen is at eye level. When your screen is too low, your neck carries the heavy weight of your head, leaving your cervical spine tired, tight, and stiff by the end of the day.
