# Wrist, Hand, and Ulnar Pain Relief

Typing on a flat keyboard without ergonomic support often causes wrist fatigue and hand soreness. Ulnar deviation—bending your wrist outward toward your pinky—can cause sharp pain or a persistent ache that travels up to the elbow. 

To fix this, keep your wrist perfectly neutral and let your elbow rest comfortably at a 90-degree angle. Stretching your hand regularly will relieve a tired wrist and reduce ulnar tightness.
