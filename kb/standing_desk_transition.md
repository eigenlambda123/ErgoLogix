# Using a Standing Desk Without New Pain

Switching to standing all day too quickly can create foot pain, low back fatigue, and calf tightness. Even healthy posture changes can overload tissues if the transition is too aggressive.

Start with short standing intervals, such as 15 to 20 minutes each hour, then progress gradually. Keep knees soft, alternate weight between legs, and use supportive footwear to reduce standing related discomfort.
