# Releasing Jaw and TMJ Workstation Tension

Stress and intense focus can make you clench your jaw without noticing, which may trigger TMJ pain, temple pressure, and neck tension. This pattern often appears during long, high concentration computer tasks.

Keep your tongue lightly on the roof of your mouth, lips closed, and teeth slightly apart to relax the jaw. Add slow breathing and a short jaw release routine to reduce clenching and facial muscle fatigue.
