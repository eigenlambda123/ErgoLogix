# Preventing Keyboard Overreach and Wrist Deviation

A straight, wide keyboard can force shoulder abduction and ulnar deviation, causing wrist pain, forearm fatigue, and shoulder tension. Reaching too far also increases neck and upper back strain.

Center your keyboard to your torso, keep elbows close, and angle input devices to maintain neutral wrists. A split keyboard with mild tenting can reduce deviation and improve comfort for heavy typing workloads.
