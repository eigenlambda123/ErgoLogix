# Easing Mid Back and Thoracic Tightness

A rounded sitting posture can overload the thoracic spine and create a dull ache between your shoulder blades. Mid back stiffness usually builds when your keyboard and monitor pull your body forward for long periods.

Bring your screen closer, sit with your ribs stacked over your hips, and keep your shoulders relaxed instead of shrugged. A short thoracic extension stretch every hour helps reduce mid back pain and restores upright posture.
