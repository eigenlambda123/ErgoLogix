# Managing Elbow and Forearm Tendon Pain

Forearm tightness and elbow pain can come from repetitive typing, gripping a mouse too hard, or unsupported wrists. Over time this strain can feel like tendon irritation near the inner or outer elbow.

Keep your forearms supported, reduce mouse grip force, and alternate high repetition tasks when possible. Gentle wrist flexor and extensor stretches plus light movement breaks can calm tendon stress and reduce forearm soreness.
