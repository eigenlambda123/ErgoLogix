# Reducing Eye Strain and Tension Headaches

Eye strain, dry eyes, and screen headaches often appear after staring at bright monitors without breaks. If your eyes feel tired, blurry, or heavy, your visual system is working too hard and your neck may also tighten.

Use the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Lower glare, increase text size, and keep the monitor about an arm length away to reduce visual fatigue and headache pressure.
