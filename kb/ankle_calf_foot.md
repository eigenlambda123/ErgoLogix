# Reducing Ankle, Calf, and Foot Discomfort

Foot ache, calf tightness, and ankle stiffness often increase when your feet do not rest flat on the floor. Unsupported legs can also lead to lower limb fatigue and restless discomfort during work.

Use a footrest if needed, keep both feet grounded, and perform ankle pumps during the day. Small mobility drills for calves and ankles improve comfort and reduce lower leg tension.
