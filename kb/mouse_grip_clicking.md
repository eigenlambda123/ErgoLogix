# Fixing Mouse Grip and Clicking Strain

Click-heavy work can overload your index finger, wrist, and forearm, especially if your mouse is too small or far from your body. This repetitive strain may feel like burning, aching, or sharp pain during long sessions.

Place the mouse close to your keyboard, keep your wrist neutral, and use your whole arm to move the pointer when possible. Lower click force, increase pointer speed, and alternate hands occasionally to reduce overuse.
