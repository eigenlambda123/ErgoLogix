# Microbreak and Stretch Routine for Desk Fatigue

Continuous focus without breaks increases total muscular fatigue and makes pain signals stronger over time. Small aches in the neck, back, wrists, and eyes often come from static load, not one single movement.

Take a 2 to 3 minute microbreak each hour for breathing, shoulder rolls, and light stretching. Consistent short resets reduce cumulative tension better than waiting for severe discomfort.
