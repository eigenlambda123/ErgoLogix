# Relieving Lower Back and Lumbar Soreness

If you experience lower back pain or lumbar soreness after long hours at your desk, your chair might be the problem. A stiff or tight lower back usually means a lack of proper lumbar support. 

To prevent a sore back, ensure your chair supports the natural curve of your spine. Taking breaks to stand and stretch will reduce fatigue and stop the dull ache associated with poor sitting habits.
