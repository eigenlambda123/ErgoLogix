# Relieving Hip Flexor and Glute Fatigue

Long desk sessions shorten your hip flexors and reduce glute activation, which can cause hip tightness, buttock soreness, and lower body fatigue. You may notice discomfort when standing up after sitting too long.

Set a timer to stand every 30 to 45 minutes, then do a brief hip flexor stretch and 10 bodyweight glute squeezes. This resets pelvic position, improves circulation, and lowers the ache from prolonged sitting.
