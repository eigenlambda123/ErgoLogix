# Improving Knee Comfort and Leg Circulation

Knee stiffness and heavy legs are common when your chair height is off or you stay still for hours. Poor circulation can create numbness, tingling, or a cramped feeling around the knees and calves.

Adjust your chair so knees are near 90 degrees with feet fully supported. Add frequent standing or short walks to restore blood flow and reduce knee pressure from static desk posture.
